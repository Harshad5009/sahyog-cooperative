Feasibility, Viability & Risk Mitigation
Is SAHYOG Deployable?
Technical, operational, and financial feasibility with proactive challenge mitigation strategies
04 / 06

✅ Technical Feasibility

Lightweight Progressive Web App — runs on any 4G/5G browser without mandatory Play Store installation. Zero native app dependencies.

✅ Operational Feasibility

Integrates with existing Labour Cooperative Societies under Maharashtra Cooperative Societies Act (1960). No new registration barrier.

✅ Financial Viability

Self-sustaining 5% ops model covers all cloud infrastructure costs without grants. Scales with transaction volume.

✅ Policy Alignment

Supports Ministry of Cooperation, e-Shram Portal, Skill India (NSDC), and ONDC Open Network. Regulatory tailwinds, not headwinds.

Challenge Identified Smart Mitigation Strategy
🔴 Worker Digital Adoption Barrier Multi-lingual voice interface (Marathi, Hindi, English) + 1-click minimalist job status transitions requiring zero form filling.
🟡 Demand vs Supply Spikes AI Predictive Workforce Advisor forecasts shortages 3 hours ahead and sends 1-click cross-zone rebalancing alerts to cooperative leads.
🟡 Commercial Platform Churn Fixed 80% guaranteed payout + ₹5 Lakh health & accidental insurance + cooperative profit dividend sharing. Workers earn more and are protected.
🟢 Customer Trust & Service Quality 100% verifiable Digital Skill Passport backed by NSDC & police clearance. Fixed rate cards and cooperative 100% satisfaction guarantee.
